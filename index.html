Verifiable History Log (VH-Log)

A general-purpose, append-only, cryptographically chained log for recording the history of a versioned state object.

The did:vh DID Method

A specialisation of VH-Log in which the state is a DID Document and the DID is just did:vh:<SCID>, with no location.
